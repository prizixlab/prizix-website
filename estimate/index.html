A quick note before you start

This estimator is designed to help you think through your project scope, priorities, and likely level of investment.

The result is only a rough planning range — not a quote or final price. Actual cost can vary significantly based on design requirements, content, integrations, migration complexity, existing systems, and other project details.

Think of it as a starting point to help you understand what may be involved before deciding what makes sense for your business.
