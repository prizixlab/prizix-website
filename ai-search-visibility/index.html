What you receive

A prioritized plan, not an unexplained score.

An assessment can lead to a one-time improvement project or ongoing work. Analysis and implementation are scoped explicitly, so you know whether the engagement includes recommendations, completed changes or both.

We can review a selection of platforms such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Grok, subject to the agreed scope and available features.

Agreed deliverables can include

  • A dated baseline of observed answers
  • Examples of inaccurate or missing information
  • A review of the sources cited in those answers
  • Prioritized website and profile corrections
  • A content plan tied to real customer questions
  • A follow-up comparison using the agreed test method

Account settings, location, wording and model changes can affect responses. A single favorable answer is not proof of broad visibility.

SEO, GEO, AEO and LLM optimization

New labels do not replace the fundamentals.

These terms are used with overlapping meanings. Our focus is concrete: accurate business information, useful content, accessible pages and documented observations of AI-assisted discovery.

For Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, Google says standard SEO practices remain relevant and no special AI markup is required. That guidance is specific to Google's search features; it is not a universal specification for every AI platform.

Realistic expectations

AI visibility questions.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No. PRIZIX does not control AI platforms, their sources or their recommendations. We can improve information you control and report observed answers, but cannot guarantee inclusion.

Is LLM optimization different from SEO?

There is substantial overlap in content quality, accessibility and clarity. AI visibility work additionally examines how selected AI answers describe or mention your business. It is not a replacement for technical SEO or useful service pages.

Can you correct a wrong answer about my business?

We can identify inaccurate source information and help correct pages or profiles you control. We can also document platform feedback options where available. Whether and when an AI platform changes its answer is outside our control.

How do you measure progress?

We compare a defined set of prompts, dates and platforms, recording mentions, citations and factual accuracy. Referral traffic and inquiries can be considered where tracking is available, but not every AI interaction produces a measurable website visit.

How quickly will results appear?

There is no reliable universal timeline. Source updates, retrieval behavior and model changes vary. We separate completed work from observed visibility changes rather than promising a result by a specific date.

What does an engagement cost?

Cost depends on the platforms and questions reviewed, the number of pages or profiles, implementation needs and any ongoing reporting. We agree on those deliverables before quoting.
