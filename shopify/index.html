Before we start

Shopify project questions.

Can you improve my existing Shopify store without rebuilding it?

Yes. We can scope focused improvements to product pages, collections, navigation, content or selected theme sections. A full redesign is appropriate only when the problems justify it.

Can you move my current website to Shopify?

We assess the current platform, available exports, product data and required features first. The proposal then identifies what will move, what needs rebuilding and what cannot transfer directly. Customer passwords, subscriptions and app-specific data can require special handling.

Will a migration preserve my Google rankings?

No one can guarantee unchanged rankings. A migration scope can include URL mapping, redirects, metadata and checks of important pages to reduce avoidable disruption. Search visibility can still fluctuate after a move.

Do you upload products and write product descriptions?

Product setup, catalog cleanup and description work can be included. We confirm the available source material, required fields, variants and level of writing before estimating the work.

How long will my project take?

The timeline depends on the agreed scope, integrations, data quality and how quickly assets and approvals are available. We provide a project-specific schedule after reviewing those requirements.

Will a redesign guarantee more sales?

No. A clearer shopping experience can address identified usability and trust issues, but sales also depend on traffic quality, products, pricing, demand and other factors. We do not promise a specific conversion increase.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short description of your goals, your current website if you have one, and any known requirements or deadlines. Product data, brand assets, access and approvals are requested as needed after the scope is agreed.
