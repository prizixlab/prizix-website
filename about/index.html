Meet Maria Vinnik

A founder with hands-on ecommerce experience.

That experience informs how PRIZIX approaches digital projects: product information, customer questions, store operations and the ability to maintain a website matter alongside its appearance.

Our working principles

Clear thinking before more technology.

Sometimes the right answer is a new website. Sometimes it is better content, fewer apps, a clearer process or fixing a small part of what already exists.

PRIZIX's role is to help identify an appropriate next step and carry out the agreed work. We define what is included, what requires further investigation and what is outside the project's control.

Specific scope

Pages, features, content responsibilities and launch requirements should be agreed—not left to assumptions.

Realistic claims

We do not guarantee a particular sales increase, Google ranking or recommendation from an AI platform.

Practical handoff

Consider how the business will use and maintain the completed work, not just what happens on launch day.
